## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-impact, uncompromising personal identity for a top-tier creative director and principal product designer. Combining the architectural discipline of Swiss Graphic Design with the raw, deliberate tension of Modern Brutalism and high-fashion editorial publishing, the aesthetic feels authoritative, avant-garde, and meticulously curated.

### Design Principles
- **Monolithic Hierarchy:** Scale contrasts are radical and intentional. Towering, ultra-condensed typography collides with hyper-refined, low-contrast structural grids.
- **Architectural Precision:** Interfaces are built on strict structural divisions, razor-sharp hairline borders, and zero-radius geometry that treat screen real estate like an exacting editorial broadsheet.
- **Atmospheric Tension:** Predominantly deep matte black and carbon surfaces punctured selectively by controlled flashes of razor-sharp crimson red, projecting gravity, discipline, and uncompromising focus.
- **Analog Humanity within Digital Rigor:** Sparse, deliberate handwritten annotations (Caveat) inject an authentic human perspective, personal critique, and directional mark-making into an otherwise austere brutalist grid.

## Colors

The palette operates on rigorous tonal isolation. It rejects soft gradients and decorative tint washes, using pure matte black, carbon tiers, and visceral crimson accents to establish dramatic visual velocity.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas (`#0A0A0A`):** Deep, non-reflective matte obsidian base. Provides limitless visual depth while letting typography dominate.
- **Surfaces (`#121212` & `#181818`):** Restrained neutral foundations for cards, data modules, timeline segments, and callouts. They differentiate structural planes without lifting the page into muddy mid-grays.
- **Primary Crimson (`#C81D1D`):** The signature editorial accent. Applied strictly to focal interactions, active states, key metric highlights, and thematic dividers.
- **Secondary Bright Crimson (`#E62E2E`):** High-energy electric red reserved for hover highlights, critical badges, focus indicators, and micro-interactive sparks.
- **Tertiary Deep Crimson (`#4A0D0D`):** Rich, blood-carbon tone used for grounded badges, border accents, and high-impact structural blocks.
- **Text & Editorial Accents:**
  - High Emphasis: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for towering headlines and essential stats.
  - Medium Emphasis: Cool Ash (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.70)`) for body narrative and metadata.
  - Low Emphasis: Industrial Muted (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.40)`) for numbering, labels, and hair-line grid indices.
  - Hairline Structural Stroke: White Wash (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).

## Typography

The typographic system creates tension through contrast: towering brutalist display cuts paired with surgical modernist sans and expressive analog notes.

### Typographic Roles
- **Display & Section Titles (Anton):** Always rendered in full uppercase with tightened vertical metrics. Anton commands the space, functioning as graphic architecture rather than passive headlines.
- **Editorial UI, Body & Metric Value Data (Plus Jakarta Sans):** High-legibility, geometric, clean. Renders critical explanations, narrative paragraphs, table data, and UI controls with crisp mechanical clarity.
- **Handwritten Signatures & Margin Notes (`Caveat`, Regular / 400, 20px–24px):** Reserved strictly for spontaneous editorial interjections—e.g., designer annotations, project year tags, curatorial notes, or marginalia around sketches. Use color `#E62E2E` or high-opacity white with a slight rotation (-2deg to -4deg) to preserve human texture.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces a strict Swiss-inspired 12-column editorial grid characterized by modular structural blocks, column-rule dividing borders, and generous negative space contrasting against dense information clusters.

### Grid & Form Factors
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with `margin-lg` (4rem) and `gutter-lg` (2rem). Content sections are framed within explicit hairline border enclosures (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`). Multi-column spans feature index numbering (e.g., `01 /`, `02 /`) positioned in the upper-left corner of cells.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns with `margin` (2rem) and `gutter` (1.5rem). High-density metric panels shift to 2-column or 4-column balanced grids.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `margin-sm` (1.25rem) and `gutter-sm` (1rem). Grid cells collapse into vertical stacked modules separated by full-bleed hairline horizontal rules.

### Spacing Strategy
Spacing enforces typographic cadence:
- Tight coupling (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) between category labels and primary headline blocks.
- Regular cadence (`space-md` to `space-lg`) within self-contained card surfaces and process milestones.
- Monumental separation (`space-xl` and above) between thematic chapters (e.g., between Hero, Case Studies, Timeline, Honors, and Contact).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately rejects ambient dropped shadows, 3D skeuomorphism, and soft blur elevations. Depth is generated strictly through **tonal planes** and **hairline spatial divisions**.

### Depth Layers
- **Ground Floor (Z-0):** Matte Black `#0A0A0A`. The primary infinite baseline canvas.
- **Structured Surfaces (Z-1):** Carbon Charcoal `#121212`. Used for content cards, data tables, and quote panels, defined strictly by 1px perimeter outlines of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- **Raised & Active Modules (Z-2):** Deep Carbon `#181818`. Used for interactive states, dropdown drawers, and elevated hover panels.
- **Overlays & Modals (Z-3):** Solid `#121212` with a 1px border of `#C81D1D` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.20)`. No backdrop blur is used; backdrops are tinted `#000000` at 85% opacity.

### Outlines & Borders
- **Standard Structural Hairlines:** `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`. Every card, column divide, and navigation bar relies on this precise boundary.
- **Active / Accent Rules:** `1px solid #C81D1D` or `2px solid #E62E2E` during hover or selection, creating immediate mechanical feedback.

## Shapes

The shape language is strictly **Sharp (0)**. There are zero border-radii across buttons, containers, cards, tags, badges, input fields, or imagery.

### Geometric Philosophy
- Every corner terminates at a clean 90-degree angle. This sharp geometry anchors the Swiss Brutalist aesthetic, treating screens as physical sheets of grid paper, architectural plans, and typography-first posters.
- Avatars, thumbnail masks, and modal dialogues are uncompromisingly square or rectangular.
- Hover indicators manifest as rectangular color fills, hairline underline shifts, or sharp perimeter borders rather than rounded pills.

## Components

### 1. Buttons & Action Links
- **Primary Button:** Rectangular, background `#C81D1D`, text `#FFFFFF`, typography `label-lg` (uppercase). Padding: `14px 28px`. Hover: Background shifts to `#E62E2E` with a subtle +2px rightwards arrow translation. No border-radius.
- **Secondary (Ghost) Button:** Rectangular, background `transparent`, 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover: Border color becomes `#C81D1D` and text shifts to `#FFFFFF` over `#121212`.
- **Editorial Action Link:** Inline text, uppercase `label-md`, followed by `→` or `↗`. Hover triggers an immediate color swap from ash white to `#E62E2E`.

### 2. Project Cards
- **Structure:** Modular grid cell with `#121212` fill and 1px hairline border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- **Media Asset:** Edge-to-edge sharp rectangular container with high-contrast desaturated imagery. On hover, imagery gains subtle crimson tonal warmth (`mix-blend-mode: hard-light` or filtered overlay).
- **Metadata Area:** Top row contains Roman or numerical indexing (e.g., `[ 01 ]`), year, and industry tags in `label-sm`. Anton headline in `headline-md`, followed by concise brief in `body-md`.

### 3. Structured KPI Metrics
- **Layout:** Monolithic 4-column split grid with hairline dividing vertical rules.
- **Data Stat:** Number set in Anton (`display-hero-mobile` to `headline-xl`) in pure `#FFFFFF` or `#C81D1D`.
- **Description:** Two-line label in uppercase `label-md` (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.40)`).

### 4. Work Process Timeline
- **Format:** Linear vertical rail or horizontal multi-step grid. Hairline rule connects steps.
- **Step Markers:** Solid sharp square markers (8px x 8px) in `#C81D1D`.
- **Milestone Units:** Phase title in `headline-sm`, accompanying breakdown in `body-md`, and handwritten annotations (`Caveat`) pointing to unique reflections or methodology footnotes.

### 5. Quote Block
- **Format:** Inset `#181818` slab with a prominent vertical left border (3px solid `#C81D1D`).
- **Typography:** Statement rendered in `body-xl` with pure white color.
- **Citation:** Name and title set in uppercase `label-md` accompanied by an italicized or Caveat script role indicator.

### 6. Honors & Recognition List
- **Table Structure:** Border-collapse table or stack of rows separated by 1px hairline rules (`rgba(255,255,255,0.12)`).
- **Row Anatomy:** Year (`label-sm`), Project (`headline-sm`), Awarding Body (`body-md`), and Category Badge.
- **Hover Reaction:** Row background turns to `#181818` with the award title shifting to `#E62E2E`.

### 7. Chips & Filter Tags
- **Style:** Zero-radius geometric boxes. Padding `6px 12px`.
- **Inactive:** Transparent background, 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`, text `label-sm` in ash white.
- **Active:** Background `#4A0D0D`, border `1px solid #C81D1D`, text `#FFFFFF`.

### 8. Input Fields & Contact Showcase
- **Inputs:** Crisp `#0A0A0A` background with a bottom-only or full 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.20)`). Placeholder in muted white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.30)`). Focus state transitions border to `1px solid #E62E2E` with zero glow.
- **Contact Card:** Massive Anton contact headline (`LET'S TALK / START A PROJECT`) accompanied by direct copy-paste email interaction block, timezone status indicator (with flashing green/crimson 6px square beacon), and direct socials list.
